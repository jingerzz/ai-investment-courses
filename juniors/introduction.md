# Introduction: Why This Matters

---

You've probably heard people talk about AI and investing. Maybe you've
even asked ChatGPT about a stock and gotten an answer that sounded
smart — but was based on information from two years ago. The AI had
no idea what the stock price actually was. It just guessed.

That's the problem this course solves.

AI is really good at reading data and explaining what it means. But by
default, it can't look up a stock price, check how a company is doing,
or compare two investments. It's like having a really smart friend who
hasn't checked the news in two years.

In this course, you'll build tools that connect AI to real stock market
data. When you're done, you'll be able to ask Claude things like "How
is Roblox doing today?" and get an answer based on the actual current
stock price — not a guess.

---

## What You'll Actually Build

Over four weeks, you'll create a set of AI-powered tools:

- A **stock tracker** that lets Claude look up real prices for companies
  you care about (Roblox, Spotify, Snapchat, Duolingo, Crocs — whatever you want)
- A **daily report tool** with safety checks that keep the AI from
  making math mistakes or showing you old data without telling you
- A **system design** for connecting multiple AI tools together
- A **stock monitor** that watches your picks and alerts you when
  something big happens

If you do the optional bonus project, you'll also build a **private
document reader** that can answer questions about a company's annual
report — running entirely on your own computer.

These aren't fake examples. They use real stock data. The tools you
build will keep working after the course is over.

---

## You Don't Need to Know How to Code

Here's the most important thing: **you won't write any code yourself.**

You'll use a tool called Claude Code. You tell it what you want in
regular English, and it writes all the code for you. Your job is to:

1. **Describe** what you want clearly (Week 1 teaches this)
2. **Check** whether what Claude built is correct (Week 2 teaches this)
3. **Fix things** when the first version isn't right (every week practices this)
4. **Think big** about what's possible (Weeks 3-4 teach this)

Think of it like being a movie director. You don't operate the camera
yourself — you tell the crew what you want, watch the result, and say
"that's great" or "let's try it differently." Claude is your crew.

---

## How Each Week Works

Every week has two parts:

**Reading (30 minutes):** Learn the concepts. What are stocks? How does
AI connect to data? Why can't you trust AI to do math? The reading
explains ideas you'll need, without assuming you already know them.

**Exercise (30 minutes):** Build something with Claude Code. You'll open
your terminal (a text window on your computer), start Claude Code, and
describe what you want to build. A conversation guide shows you exactly
what to type. A checklist helps you verify your work. A reference
solution shows what a good result looks like.

Each week builds on the last. Week 1's stock tracker becomes the
foundation for Week 2's safety checks. Week 3 plans a bigger system.
Week 4 adds monitoring. By the end, you've built a complete set of
connected tools.

## Who This Course Is For

You're a high school student who's curious about two things: **investing**
and **AI**. Maybe you've already started investing with a custodial
account. Maybe you just follow stocks for fun. Maybe you're interested
in AI and want to see what it can actually do beyond chatbots.

You don't need to know anything about programming, Python, servers, or
machine learning. You don't even need to know much about the stock
market — we'll explain the basics as we go.

What you do need: curiosity, willingness to try new things on your
computer, and about an hour a week for four weeks.

---

## A Quick Note About Real Money

This course teaches you to build tools that show real stock data. That's
cool, but it's important to understand: **these tools are for learning,
not for making real investment decisions.**

Professional investors use many more data sources, years of experience,
and strict risk management. The tools in this course are a starting
point for understanding how AI and finance work together — not a
shortcut to picking winners.

If you're interested in actual investing, talk to a parent or guardian
about custodial brokerage accounts, and remember that all investing
involves risk. The best investment you can make at your age is in your
own education — which is exactly what you're doing right now.
