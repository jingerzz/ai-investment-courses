# Foundations 2: Conversation Guide -- Cross-Platform Workflows

Example prompts organized by scenario. Each includes a note on which
platform works best. Adapt these to the stocks and topics you're
interested in.

---

## Quick Questions on Mobile

Best on mobile -- when you have a few minutes between classes or on
the go.

**Check on a stock idea:**
```
Remind me -- what were the main pros and cons we talked about
for Apple as an investment?
```

**Prep for stock club:**
```
I have a stock club meeting after school. Give me three talking
points based on the research we did on [Company] this week.
```

**Capture a thought:**
```
I just heard that [Company] is launching a new product. Can you
help me think about whether that's good or bad for their stock?
We can do deeper research later on desktop.
```

**Quick concept check:**
```
Can you explain what "market cap" means in one sentence? I keep
seeing it but I'm not sure I fully get it.
```

---

## Research Sessions on Desktop

Best on desktop -- use attachments, longer conversations, and detailed
follow-up.

**News article analysis:**
```
[Attach news article PDF or screenshot]
What does this article say about the company's future? What's
positive and what's concerning for investors?
```

**Stock chart review:**
```
[Attach chart screenshot from Yahoo Finance]
Describe what you see in this chart. Has the stock been going up
or down? Are there any patterns you notice?
```

**Comparing two companies:**
```
I'm trying to decide between investing in Apple and Nike for
a school project. Help me compare them. What are the strengths
and weaknesses of each as an investment?
```

**Understanding a company:**
```
[Attach a page from a company's website or annual report]
Help me understand what this company actually does and how they
make money. Break it down simply.
```

## After Stock Club on Mobile

Best on mobile -- capture ideas while they're fresh.

**Meeting notes:**
```
Here are my notes from today's stock club meeting:
[type or dictate your notes]

Organize these into:
1. Stocks we decided to watch
2. Stocks we decided to research more
3. Questions we need to answer next time
```

**Save an idea for later:**
```
Someone in the club mentioned that [Company] might be a good
investment because [reason]. I want to research this on desktop
later. What should I look into?
```

**Reflect on a pick:**
```
Our club picked [Stock] three weeks ago and it's down 8%. Help
me think about whether our original reasoning was wrong or if
this is just normal volatility.
```

---

## Learning and Study on Desktop

Best on desktop -- longer, more thoughtful conversations.

**Understand a concept:**
```
[Attach class notes or textbook page]
I'm learning about P/E ratios but I'm confused about the part
on this page. Can you explain it differently and give me a
real example with a company I know?
```

**Build a watchlist:**
```
I want to start tracking 5 stocks in different industries.
Help me pick a diverse set and tell me what I should pay
attention to for each one.
```

**Practice analysis:**
```
[Attach a news article about any company]
Pretend I'm an analyst and quiz me: what questions should I
be asking after reading this article? Then tell me if my
answers are on the right track.
```

**Weekly review:**
```
This week I learned about [topic]. Here's my understanding:
[explain in your own words]

Am I getting this right? What am I missing or
misunderstanding?
```
