# Foundations 2 Exercise: Setting Up Your Stock Research Workspace

## What You'll Do

You'll install Claude on your computer (and optionally your phone),
set up projects for your stock research, and practice using attachments
with a real document. By the end, you'll have a workspace that's ready
for Week 1 -- when you'll connect Claude Desktop to a live stock tool.

## Time: 20 minutes

## What You Need

- A Mac or PC
- Your Anthropic account (the same one you used on claude.ai)
- A document to test with -- a news article about a company, a
  screenshot of a stock chart, or even class notes
- Optional: an iPhone or Android phone

---

- [ ] Download and install the app for your computer.

## Step 3: Try the Attachment Workflow (5 min)

Find a document on your computer to share with Claude. Good options:

- A news article about a company (save as PDF or screenshot it)
- A screenshot of a stock chart from Yahoo Finance or Google Finance
- Class notes or a handout related to investing or economics

Drag the document into a Claude Desktop conversation (or click the
paperclip icon). Then try these prompts:

**Prompt 1 -- Summarize:**
```
Summarize the key points of this document in 5 bullet points.
What would a student investor want to know?
```

**Prompt 2 -- Dig deeper:**
```
Based on this document, what are two things that could be good
for investors and two things that could be risky?
```

**Prompt 3 -- Connect it:**
```
If I were thinking about buying stock in this company, what's the
most important thing in this document I should pay attention to?
```

Notice how Claude's answers reference the actual content of your
document -- not generic information. That's the difference between
asking "tell me about Apple" and giving Claude a specific article
about Apple to read.

- [ ] I successfully uploaded a document to Claude Desktop.
- [ ] Claude's response referenced specific details from my document.

## Step 4: Find the Developer Menu (2 min)

This is simple but important:

- [ ] In Claude Desktop, open **Settings** (gear icon).
- [ ] Find the **Developer** section.
- [ ] Look at the MCP server configuration area. It should be empty.

You don't need to change anything here. But in Week 1, this is exactly
where you'll paste a configuration that connects Claude to a live
stock tool. When you do, Claude will be able to answer questions like
"What's today's market signal?" using real computed data instead of
its training knowledge.

Knowing where this menu lives saves you time next week.

- [ ] I found Settings > Developer and saw the MCP configuration area.

## Step 5: Create Your Projects (5 min)

Create at least 2 projects with custom instructions. Use the templates
from the reading, or write your own.

### Required: "Stock Research"

- [ ] Create a new project called "Stock Research."
- [ ] Add custom instructions (use the template from the reading or
  write your own -- the key is telling Claude you're a student
  investor and how you want it to help).
- [ ] Start a test conversation: ask Claude about a company you're
  interested in and see how it responds within this project.

### Your Choice: Pick One More

- [ ] Create a second project. Choose one:
  - **"School Stock Club"** -- if you're in a club or group project
  - **"Learning Journal"** -- if you want to track what you learn
  - **Something else** -- a project that fits your situation

- [ ] Add custom instructions to your second project.
- [ ] Start a test conversation in it to make sure the instructions
