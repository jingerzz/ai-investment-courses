# Checklist: Foundations 1

Use this to make sure you've got the key concepts down and finished
the hands-on exercise. Every item should be a confident "yes" before
you move on to Foundations 2.

---

## Concepts

- [ ] **Can you explain what a large language model is?**
  One sentence: a reasoning tool trained on text that has no access
  to live data -- like a study partner who read every book but has
  no phone.

- [ ] **Can you name the 5 ways AI gets things wrong?**
  Stale magnitude, confabulated facts, hallucinated reasoning,
  numerical plausibility, and no uncertainty signal. You should be
  able to give an example of each.

- [ ] **Do you understand the context window?**
  Claude's working memory: about 150,000 words (two Harry Potter
  books). Long conversations eventually lose early messages.
  Specific questions get better answers.

- [ ] **Can you explain what custom instructions do?**
  They tell Claude who you are and how you want it to respond.
  Set them once, and they apply to every conversation you start.

- [ ] **Do you know the difference between a plain conversation
  and a project?**
  Projects have their own instructions and uploaded documents.
  Different projects can serve different purposes (stock research
  vs. school club vs. learning).

## Hands-On

- [ ] **Did you ask Claude for a stock price and check it?**
  You asked for Tesla's price (or another stock), looked up the
  real price, and noticed the difference.

- [ ] **Have you created a Claude project?**
  You have a project called "Stock Research" (or similar) with
  instructions that describe your interests and level.

- [ ] **Have you written custom instructions?**
  Either in your profile settings or your project, you wrote
  instructions that tell Claude you're a student learning about
  investing.

- [ ] **Have you uploaded a document to project knowledge?**
  You added a news article or other document and asked Claude
  questions about it.

- [ ] **Did you try pushing back on Claude's answer?**
  You pointed out something Claude missed or got wrong and asked
  it to redo the analysis.

## Ready for Foundations 2

- [ ] **You're comfortable navigating claude.ai.** You can start
  conversations, switch between projects, and find your uploaded
  documents.

- [ ] **You understand that AI fails silently.** You know that
  Claude presents wrong information with the same confidence as
  correct information, and that verification is your job.

- [ ] **You've started to develop a feel for good prompts.** You
  know that specific questions with clear direction get better
  answers than vague ones.

---

If any items above are unchecked, revisit that section of the
reading or the exercise before continuing. Foundations 2 builds
directly on these skills.
