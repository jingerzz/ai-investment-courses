# Checklist: Foundations 1

Use this to confirm you understand the Zo operating-environment model before moving on.

## Concepts

- [ ] **You can explain Zo as more than chat.** You understand that Zo includes files, terminal, tools, hosted services, integrations, and scheduled agents.
- [ ] **You can describe the four layers.** Files, tools, surfaces, and agents each have a distinct role.
- [ ] **You understand the guardrail pattern.** AI should explain and orchestrate; deterministic tools should compute.
- [ ] **You know why this matters in finance.** Prices, filings, calculations, and audit trails need source discipline.

## Hands-On

- [ ] **You created or identified a course workspace.**
- [ ] **You drafted `workflow-map.md`.**
- [ ] **You listed real data, documents, metrics, decisions, and outputs from your investment process.**
- [ ] **You asked Zo to classify the workflow into files, scripts, services, dashboards, and agents.**
- [ ] **You identified at least two workflows that are good candidates for automation.**

## Ready for Foundations 2

- [ ] **You can explain what should never be delegated to the model.**
- [ ] **You know which parts of your workflow need exact computation or source retrieval.**
- [ ] **You have one practical workflow candidate to carry into the rest of the course.**
