# Course Brief: Zo-Centric Professional Track

## Working Title

AI-Powered Investment Management on Zo Computer

## Format

Six modules:

1. Foundations 1
2. Foundations 2
3. Week 1
4. Week 2
5. Week 3
6. Week 4

Each module contains a reading and a hands-on exercise. Expected time is 30 minutes reading plus 30 minutes hands-on.

## Primary Outcome

Students learn how to use Zo Computer as the operating environment for AI-powered investment research and workflow automation.

By the end, students should understand how to:

- Organize an investment workspace on Zo
- Use AI models against their own files and services
- Run MCP-style investment tools from a personal cloud computer
- Use SPY/TLT regime tools without hallucinating levels or signals
- Use SEC filing RAG tools with primary-source citations
- Design a multi-service investment research workflow
- Build scheduled agents with audit trails and human approval gates
