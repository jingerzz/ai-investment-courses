# Checklist: Foundations 1

Use this to confirm you've covered the key concepts and completed
the hands-on exercise. Every item should be a confident "yes" before
moving to Foundations 2.

---

## Concepts

- [ ] **Can you explain what a large language model is and isn't?**
  You should be able to describe it in one sentence: a reasoning
  engine trained on text that has no access to live data.

- [ ] **Do you understand the context window?** You know it's Claude's
  working memory (~200K tokens), that longer conversations eventually
  lose early context, and that focused questions get better answers.

- [ ] **Can you explain what custom instructions do?** You know they
  persist across messages, shape how Claude responds, and eliminate
  the need to repeat your role and preferences in every conversation.

- [ ] **Do you understand the difference between a plain conversation
  and a project?** You know that projects have their own instructions
  and knowledge base, and that different projects can serve different
  types of analysis.

## Hands-On

- [ ] **Have you created a Claude project?** You have at least one
  project with a name relevant to your work (not "Test Project").

- [ ] **Have you written custom instructions?** Your project has
  instructions that describe your role, your analytical framework,
  and your communication preferences.

- [ ] **Have you compared responses with and without instructions?**
  You asked the same question in a plain conversation and in your
  project, and you noticed the difference in framing, depth, and
  terminology.

- [ ] **Have you uploaded a document to project knowledge?** You added
  a PDF or text file and asked Claude questions about its contents.

- [ ] **Have you asked Claude to analyze your document?** Not just
  "summarize this" but a question that requires Claude to interpret
  the document through your analytical framework.

## Ready for Foundations 2

- [ ] **You're comfortable navigating claude.ai.** You can create
  conversations, switch between projects, and find your uploaded
  documents.

- [ ] **You've developed a sense for what makes a good prompt.** You
  know that specific, structured questions with clear analytical
  direction outperform vague ones.

---

If any items above are unchecked, revisit that section of the reading
or the exercise before continuing. Foundations 2 builds directly on
these skills.
