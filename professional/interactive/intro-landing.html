Build AI Tools for Investment Management — No Coding Required

Finance professionals learn to build AI-powered tools that connect to real market data, include production-grade guardrails, and work after the course ends.

The Problem

AI is powerful. But it's cut off from your data.

Today's AI models are remarkably good at reasoning, synthesis, and pattern recognition. But they don't have access to your watchlist, your risk parameters, your firm's documents, or even current market prices.

Without that connection, AI is just a very articulate colleague who hasn't read the briefing materials.

What most people do
User: "What stocks should I buy?" AI: "Based on my training data from 2024, here are some considerations..." → Confident. Generic. Stale.
What this course teaches
AI calls: get_current_signal() AI calls: get_es_levels() AI calls: get_risk_report() → "Blue regime. ES pivot 5420. Mean-reversion entry at S1. Risk: 0.45% of account."

What you'll build

Each week produces a working tool connected to real market data. The weeks build on each other into a complete system.

Week 1

Making Data AI-Accessible

Build an MCP server that gives Claude access to your stock watchlist with live prices, fundamentals, and comparisons.

Week 2

Reasoning & Guardrails

Add guardrails that prevent hallucinated prices. Build a morning briefing that cross-references multiple data sources.

Week 3

System Architecture

Design a multi-server system with separated concerns, shared libraries, and cloud deployment for your organization.

Week 4

Autonomous Agents

Build an agent that monitors, proposes, and logs — with human approval at every consequential step and a full audit trail.
