How It Works

Start with two Foundations modules that require nothing but a Claude account — learn how Claude works, set up projects, and build your workspace. Then move into four weekly modules, each following a 30+30 format: 30 minutes reading, 30 minutes hands-on.

By the end, you'll have built a complete system: pre-built tool servers, your own custom server, multi-server architecture, and an autonomous agent with approval workflows.

The system uses real market data, real SEC filings, and production-tested patterns. You can deploy it on day one.

What Makes This Different

No Coding Required

You don't need to write Python or JavaScript. You'll use Claude and standard tools to build infrastructure. If you want to extend it, you can — but you don't have to.

Real Market Data

Your servers and agents connect to real stock prices, SEC filings, and portfolio data. You'll see guardrails work on real-world complexity, not toy examples.

Production Patterns

Multi-server architecture, approval workflows, audit trails, and compliance controls aren't afterthoughts — they're designed in from week one. You'll understand why institutions build systems this way.

Cumulative Build

Foundations teaches you Claude. Week 1 gives you hands-on experience with pre-built AI tools. Week 2 teaches you to build your own. Week 3 combines them into architecture. Week 4 adds autonomy and controls. Each module builds on the previous one.

Choose Your Track
