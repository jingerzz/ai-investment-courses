The Tool-Use Pattern

How AI connects to your data through structured tools — not data dumps.

Step 1
User Asks
"How is AAPL performing in my portfolio?"
Step 2
AI Reads Tools
get_position_summary(ticker: str) → Position details including P&L, weight, risk
Step 3
AI Decides
I need position data for AAPL → call get_position_summary("AAPL")
Step 4
Tool Executes
{ "ticker": "AAPL", "shares": 150, "avg_cost": 142.5, "current_price": 178.32, "unrealized_pnl": 5373.0, "return_pct": 25.13, "portfolio_weight_pct": 12.4, "data_source": "live_feed", "as_of": "2026-03-07T16:00:00Z" }
Step 5
AI Synthesizes
AAPL is your largest position at 12.4% of the portfolio. You're up $5,373 (+25.1%) on 150 shares. This concentration warrants monitoring given current market conditions.

Why This Beats Data Dumps

X Traditional: Dump Everything
X AI receives entire database export (50,000+ rows)
X AI searches through noise for relevant data
X Context window consumed by irrelevant information
X High latency, context limits, poor synthesis
Inefficient Context waste Slow Error-prone
✓ Tool-Use: Structured Calls
✓ AI identifies the right function for the task
✓ Calls get_position_summary("AAPL")
✓ Receives only the data needed (5 key fields)
✓ Fast, focused, accurate synthesis
Efficient Focused Fast Reliable
The Mental Model

Think of AI as a new analyst who just joined your desk. They're smart — but they need access to your systems. MCP tools are the equivalent of giving them a login to your terminals.
