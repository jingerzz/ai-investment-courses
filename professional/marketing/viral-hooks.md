# 9 Viral Hooks — 3-Second Scripts with Visual Storyboards

> **Target audience:** Investment industry professionals (analysts, PMs, IT managers at investment firms)
> **Core provocation:** People who know how to *build* with AI will displace those who just *use* it. And "building" no longer requires code.

---

## Group A — "You're Already Behind" (Fear / Urgency)

### Hook 1
**Script:** "Your junior analyst just built a trading system. In 30 minutes. Without code."

**Visual direction:**
- Screen recording style: terminal window with Claude Code open
- Text appears character-by-character as if being typed: "Build me an MCP server that monitors my watchlist..."
- Cut to: a working dashboard pulling live market data
- Speed: fast cuts, high energy

**Text overlay:** `30 MINUTES. NO CODE. LIVE MARKET DATA.`

**Platform:** TikTok / Instagram Reels (vertical, fast-paced)

---

### Hook 2
**Script:** "The analyst who replaces you won't know more about markets. They'll know more about AI."

**Visual direction:**
- Split screen: Left side shows traditional analyst at Bloomberg terminal, Right side shows someone in plain clothes typing plain English into a terminal
- Right side produces the same output faster
- Left side person looks over, concerned

**Text overlay:** `It's not about markets anymore.`

**Platform:** LinkedIn Video / TikTok (works both vertical and horizontal)

---

### Hook 3
**Script:** "Someone at your firm is already doing this. They just haven't told you yet."

**Visual direction:**
- POV: walking through a finance office, camera passes cubicles
- One person's screen briefly visible — it shows Claude Code with MCP server output
- Camera lingers, then keeps walking
- Tension / mystery vibe

**Text overlay:** `Are you the one building? Or the one being replaced?`

**Platform:** Instagram Reels / TikTok (vertical, cinematic)

---

## Group B — "This Isn't What You Think" (Reframe)

### Hook 4
**Script:** "If you think 'using AI' means talking to ChatGPT — you're already two years behind."

**Visual direction:**
- Close-up: someone typing "What stocks should I buy?" into ChatGPT
- Red X appears over the screen
- Cut to: someone typing architectural descriptions into Claude Code, building a multi-server system
- Green checkmark

**Text overlay:** `Talking to AI ≠ Building with AI`

**Platform:** All platforms (universal hook)

---

### Hook 5
**Script:** "AI won't replace portfolio managers. Portfolio managers who build with AI will."

**Visual direction:**
- Text-on-screen format (dark background, white text)
- First line appears: "AI won't replace portfolio managers."
- Pause. Then second line slams in: "Portfolio managers who build with AI will."
- Quick montage: hands typing plain English → working agent → Telegram approval notification → trade executed

**Text overlay:** The hook IS the text overlay. Clean, typographic.

**Platform:** LinkedIn Video (professional tone, horizontal)

---

### Hook 6
**Script:** "The gap isn't AI vs. no AI. It's building tools vs. asking questions."

**Visual direction:**
- Two-panel animation:
  - Left panel labeled "ASKING" — chat bubble: "What's the market outlook?" → generic paragraph response
  - Right panel labeled "BUILDING" — terminal: building MCP server → live market data → morning briefing → trade proposal with risk calculations
- Right panel keeps growing while left stays static

**Text overlay:** `Which side are you on?`

**Platform:** TikTok / Instagram Reels (vertical, animated)

---

## Group C — "The New Skill Stack" (Aspiration / Concrete)

### Hook 7
**Script:** "Morning briefing. Risk check. Trade proposal. All built by someone who can't code."

**Visual direction:**
- Quick cuts showing real tool output:
  - Cut 1: Morning briefing markdown rendering with signal colors and price levels
  - Cut 2: Risk report showing position sizing and exposure
  - Cut 3: Telegram notification with approve/reject buttons
- End card: person smiling, closing laptop

**Text overlay:** `Built in plain English. Runs on real data.`

**Platform:** LinkedIn Video / TikTok

---

### Hook 8
**Script:** "30 minutes. Plain English. A working MCP server connected to live market data."

**Visual direction:**
- Timer in corner counting from 0:00
- Timelapse of someone describing a system in Claude Code
- Timer hits 30:00 — cut to: the working server returning live stock data
- Record scratch / satisfying sound effect

**Text overlay:** `From idea to production tool. Half an hour.`

**Platform:** TikTok / YouTube Shorts (vertical, timelapse format)

---

### Hook 9
**Script:** "Your compliance team would actually approve this. That's the hard part — and we teach it."

**Visual direction:**
- Shows an agent proposing a trade via Telegram-style notification
- Zooms into the details: audit trail, approval levels, escalation rules, logged reasoning
- Cut to: a compliance officer nodding (humorous exaggeration)
- Subtle flex: "This is from a production trading platform"

**Text overlay:** `Guardrails. Audit trails. Human approval. The stuff that actually matters.`

**Platform:** LinkedIn Video (professional audience, compliance angle)
